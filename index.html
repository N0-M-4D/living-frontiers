Field manual

Command with confidence.

Select troops
Left-click a blue company or drag a box. Shift adds or removes companies.
Move / retreat from combat
Right-click ground. Troops move without stopping to shoot. Shift + right-click queues another waypoint. Right-drag spreads selected companies along a line; release to confirm.
Attack
Right-click an enemy. Or press A, then click ground for attack-move.
Cancel
Escape or right-click cancels an active placement or targeting order, keeping your troops selected. Escape again clears selection.
Camera
Scroll to zoom. Hold Space and drag, or middle-drag, to pan.
Build & artillery
Build → choose a named building → click valid ground. Click an existing building to recruit or aim its battery.
Rotate trenches
During placement, R rotates 15 degrees; Shift+R rotates back. The cover area follows the trench.
Shortcuts
M move · A attack-move · H hold · F artillery · P pause · 1–9 select companies
